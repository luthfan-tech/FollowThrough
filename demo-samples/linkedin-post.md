# 🚀 LinkedIn Post Draft for the Agentic AI Hackathon

---

Most AI meeting tools are building the wrong thing.

They give you a 5-page transcript summary that nobody reads.
And what happens next?
- Commitments get forgotten in Slack threads.
- Teams make decisions that directly contradict policies agreed upon two weeks ago.
- High-priority tasks are left with "the team" as the owner (which means nobody owns it).
- And engineers burn out under unrealistic, overnight deadlines.

For the **Agentic AI Hackathon**, my teammate and I set out to solve this:

💡 **“Meetings create decisions. FollowThrough makes sure they get executed.”**

Introducing **FollowThrough** ⚡ — an autonomous, human-in-the-loop Agentic AI execution engine that bridges the gap between conversational consensus and tracked organizational work.

Here is why FollowThrough is radically different from another "meeting summarizer":

🧠 **1. Persistent Organizational Decision Memory**
Every ratified decision, architectural standard (ADR), and public commitment is indexed in persistent memory across meetings.

🚨 **2. The Conflict Radar**
When a new meeting takes place, the agent doesn't just parse text—it actively compares current proposals against historical memory.
- Did the team decide to drop iOS 15 support? 🛑 *Conflict Radar catches that ADR-24 mandated iOS 15+ to protect 28% of enterprise customers.*
- Did an engineer propose swapping our auth stack over the weekend? 🛑 *Conflict Radar flags a security governance violation.*
- Are tasks unassigned or due in under 18 hours? 🛑 *Conflict Radar flags velocity traps and missing DRIs before work begins.*

🛡️ **3. Mandatory Human-in-the-Loop Confirmation Gate**
AI agents shouldn't blindly write to production tracking systems. FollowThrough stages proposed decisions, tasks, and reminders for explicit human confirmation. You approve, edit, or reject each item.

📊 **4. Autonomous Task Dispatch & Weekly Digests**
Once confirmed:
- Tasks automatically dispatch to the Kanban board / Linear sync.
- Proactive reminders schedule ahead of deadlines.
- Personalized **Weekly Digests** generate for each team member (Marcus, Elena, Dev Priya, Sam) highlighting deliverables due, overdue blockers, and conflicts requiring their attention.

📈 **5. The Org Execution Score (0–100)**
A transparent KPI measuring decision clarity, ownership coverage, deadline precision, and conflict density across the company.

🛠️ **Tech Stack**:
- Next.js 14+ (App Router, Server Actions, Route Handlers)
- TypeScript & Tailwind CSS (Custom Dark-Mode SaaS UI)
- Hybrid AI Engine: Gemini 1.5 Flash structured JSON schema extraction + fallback deterministic reasoning engine
- Persistent ACID storage & SQLite data models
- Zero-external-setup instant local demo

We built this end-to-end to prove that the future of agentic AI isn't passive chatbots—it's **active, accountable execution**.
