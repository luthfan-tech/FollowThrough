# 🎬 FollowThrough: 60-90 Second Hackathon Demo Video Script

**Target Duration**: 75–90 seconds  
**Tone**: Fast-paced, confident, technical, product-focused.  
**Speaker**: Founder / Lead Product Engineer  

---

### [0:00 - 0:12] The Hook: The Core Problem
*(Visual: Screen begins on FollowThrough's sleek dark-mode Dashboard showing the Org Execution Score and Conflict Radar alerts.)*

> **Voiceover:**  
> "How many times has your engineering team spent an hour in a meeting, agreed on critical decisions, only for someone to accidentally contradict a past policy, leave high-priority tasks with no owner, or watch commitments evaporate into Slack silence?  
> Most AI meeting tools are just passive summarizers. Meet **FollowThrough**: the autonomous execution agent that turns meeting transcripts into confirmed, tracked work."

---

### [0:12 - 0:28] Ingestion & Multi-Step Agent Pipeline
*(Visual: Click 'Ingest Meeting' -> Select Sample 1: "Q3 Mobile App Launch Sync" -> Click 'Run FollowThrough Agent'.)*

> **Voiceover:**  
> "Watch what happens. We ingest a raw transcript from today’s mobile sync. In less than two seconds, FollowThrough attributes the speakers, infers their organizational roles, and extracts ratified decisions and action items.  
> But here’s where FollowThrough is radically different: **Decision Memory** and the **Conflict Radar**."

---

### [0:28 - 0:50] The Breakthrough: Conflict Radar & Memory Contradiction
*(Visual: Zoom in on the Agent Activity Timeline, then highlight the glowing red CRITICAL CONTRADICTION card.)*

> **Voiceover:**  
> "The agent queries our persistent organizational memory and immediately halts:  
> **Critical Contradiction detected!**  
> In today’s sync, the team decided to drop iOS 15 support. But FollowThrough cross-checks ADR-24 ratified two weeks ago, showing that iOS 15 support was strictly mandated to protect 28% of enterprise customers!  
> It also flags a developer trying to swap Supabase for Auth0 without an architecture review, and warns that the release date was shifted without marketing sign-off."

---

### [0:50 - 1:08] Human-in-the-Loop Confirmation Gate
*(Visual: Hover over the proposed items. Toggle 'Rejected' on the Auth0 migration, keep the valid tasks, then show the sticky bottom confirmation bar.)*

> **Voiceover:**  
> "Notice: **FollowThrough never blindly pushes changes.**  
> Our mandatory **Human-in-the-Loop Confirmation Gate** guarantees that nothing enters Jira, Linear, or long-term memory without explicit human approval.  
> We can reject the unauthorized auth change, approve the safe deliverables, and click **Confirm & Execute**."

---

### [1:08 - 1:25] Autonomous Dispatch & Weekly Digests
*(Visual: Click 'Confirm & Execute'. Watch the success banner appear, navigate to the Kanban Task Board showing the created tasks, then click 'Weekly Digest'.)*

> **Voiceover:**  
> "Instantly, FollowThrough commits the verified decisions into organizational memory, dispatches approved tasks to the Kanban board, schedules proactive deadline reminders, and generates personalized **Weekly Digests** for each team member—giving everyone their exact priorities, overdue blockers, and conflict warnings."

---

### [1:25 - 1:35] Closing CTA
*(Visual: Return to the Executive Dashboard showing the updated Execution Score.)*

> **Voiceover:**  
> "Meetings create decisions. FollowThrough makes sure they get executed.  
> Check out the live demo and runnable codebase on GitHub. Thank you!"
